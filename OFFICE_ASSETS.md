# Office game art

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Both final PNG atlases have genuine alpha transparency. The game reads their alpha bounds and scales visible silhouettes without changing source pixels.

Final assets: [office props](public/office-props-hatched.png) and [minimal spiked work symbols](public/work-symbols-hatched.png).

Office props: 2×2, desk / chair / cabinet / printer. Work symbols: 3×2, mail / KPI pressure / deadline / meeting / overtime / cursor. All work symbols render at the same 68-pixel maximum visible dimension.
